# Data Dictionary

## AIRPAC-Q

Population: 528 Chinese pre-service teachers.

Core constructs:
- AI literacy
- Risk perception
- Academic confidence
- Future AI-use intention
- Prior AI use
- Demographic covariates

## Vietnam ECE

Population: 426 in-service early-childhood teachers.

Core constructs:
- Capability
- Professional-development usefulness
- Platform value
- Privacy and security
- Human oversight
- AI acceptance
- Barriers and impediments
- Prior tool use
- Training needs
- Sharing conditions

## Direct Chinese evidence

Five empirical studies involving preschool or kindergarten personnel in China. Extracted fields include study characteristics, sample, constructs, measures, reported statistics, principal findings, and mapped 4C domains.
