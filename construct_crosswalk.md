# Construct Crosswalk

| 4C domain | AIRPAC-Q | Vietnam ECE | Direct Chinese evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competence | AI literacy | Capability | AI competence, skills, and operational ability |
| Confidence | Academic confidence | AI acceptance and perceived platform value | Confidence, self-efficacy, and positive attitude |
| Caution | Risk perception | Privacy, security, and human oversight | Ethical, safety, and governance concerns |
| Context | Prior AI use | Professional-development benefit, barriers, and platform environment | Institutional, policy, resource, and leadership support |

## Interpretation note

The crosswalk supports construct-level synthesis only. It does not imply identical measurement instruments or measurement equivalence across evidence streams.
